# Product Vision — Ideation to Value

## Intent
Build an inclusive, client-agnostic system linking problems, ideas, strategic objectives, value streams, architecture decisions, experiments, implementation and evidenced outcomes.

## Actors
- Business Line (BL): submits and sponsors a problem or opportunity.
- Enterprise Architecture Office (EAO): assesses architecture alignment, options, dependencies and risk.
- Value Realization (VR): tests benefit hypotheses, baselines, attribution and realized value.
- Implementation Arm (IA): owns delivery feasibility, work packages, milestones and acceptance evidence.
- Portfolio Council: decides funding, deferral, termination and scaling.
- Learner: conducts supervised synthetic innovation simulations.

## SuccessLabs six engines
Knowledge: reuse decisions and cases. Attention: challenge discovery and showcases. Revenue: diagnostic products and enterprise innovation offers. Transformation: idea-to-outcome delivery. Operating: governance and operational clarity. Leadership: scenario-based executive decisions.

## Product principles
1. Problem before proposed solution.
2. Every proposed value has assumptions and measurable baselines.
3. Separate forecast benefits from audited actuals.
4. Capture architecture trade-offs and dissenting opinions.
5. Human approval at material decision gates.
6. Privacy-first demonstrations and tenant-level controls in future production.
7. Allow experiments that disprove hypotheses; celebrate learning, not only approvals.

## Stage gates
Discover: problem framing; Evaluate: strategic and feasibility screening; Architect: alternative designs and review; Experiment: hypothesis and test evidence; Deliver: release and operational adoption; Realize: verified benefit tracking and lessons learned.
